What Is CapCut for PC?

CapCut is a video editing software developed by ByteDance. Its desktop version is designed for computer users who prefer editing videos on a larger screen with a keyboard and mouse.

Users can import video clips, add music, insert text, apply transitions, adjust colors, and export their finished projects.
