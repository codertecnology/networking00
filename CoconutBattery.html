coconutBattery: A Powerful Tool to Check Your MacBook Battery Health

coconutBattery is a popular battery monitoring application for Mac users. It helps users check the health, capacity, charging status, and overall condition of their device batteries.

If you own a MacBook, iPhone, or iPad, understanding battery health is important for maintaining your device. coconutBattery provides useful battery information in one simple interface, helping you understand how your battery performs over time.

What Is coconutBattery?

coconutBattery is a macOS utility that displays live battery information for Mac computers and connected Apple devices. It provides details such as current charge, full charge capacity, original design capacity, and battery cycle count.

The application also helps users save battery health information so they can monitor changes in battery capacity over time. This makes it especially useful for people who want to understand battery aging.

Key Features of coconutBattery

1. Battery Health Monitoring

One of the main features of coconutBattery is battery health monitoring. It compares the current full charge capacity with the original design capacity to help users understand how much battery capacity remains.

2. Battery Cycle Count

coconutBattery displays the battery cycle count of supported devices. This information helps users understand how frequently a battery has been charged and discharged.

3. Battery Capacity Information

The application shows important battery capacity details, including current charge capacity and original design capacity. These measurements help users understand battery performance.

4. iPhone and iPad Battery Information

coconutBattery can also read battery information from connected iPhones and iPads. Users can connect their devices to a Mac and view available battery details.

5. Battery Temperature Monitoring

Another useful feature is battery temperature monitoring. Users can check available temperature information and better understand their device's battery conditions.

6. Battery History

coconutBattery allows users to save battery health information. This helps them compare previous readings and understand how battery capacity changes over time.

7. Menu Bar Information

Users can access useful battery information through the macOS menu bar. This makes checking battery status more convenient without opening the full application.
