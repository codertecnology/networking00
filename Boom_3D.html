What Is Boom 3D?

Boom 3D is a sound enhancement software developed to provide a more immersive audio experience. It uses audio processing technology to create a virtual surround sound effect and improve the way users hear audio through compatible headphones and speakers.

The application also provides sound customization options, allowing users to adjust audio according to their personal preferences.
