Mac HEIC Converter: How to Convert HEIC Images on Mac

If you use an Apple device, you may already know about the HEIC image format. HEIC is commonly used on iPhones because it provides high-quality images while taking up less storage space. However, HEIC images are not always compatible with every application or device.

A Mac HEIC Converter can help you convert HEIC images into popular formats such as JPG and PNG. This makes sharing, editing, and opening your pictures easier.

What Is a Mac HEIC Converter?

A Mac HEIC Converter is a tool that changes images from High Efficiency Image Container (HEIC) format into other image formats. The most popular conversion options include JPG, JPEG, and PNG.

These formats are widely supported by websites, image editors, Windows computers, and other devices.

Why Do You Need to Convert HEIC Images on Mac?

Although HEIC offers excellent image quality and smaller file sizes, some platforms and applications may not support it. Converting HEIC files can help solve these compatibility issues.

  • Make images compatible with more devices.
  • Share pictures more easily.
  • Upload images to websites that require JPG or PNG.
  • Edit images in applications that do not support HEIC.
  • Use converted images in documents and presentations.

How to Convert HEIC to JPG on Mac

You can convert HEIC images on your Mac using the built-in Preview application. Follow these simple steps:

  1. Find the HEIC image on your Mac.
  2. Right-click the image and select Open With.
  3. Choose Preview.
  4. Click File and select Export.
  5. Choose JPEG from the Format menu.
  6. Select your desired image quality.
  7. Click Save to complete the conversion.

2. Can I convert HEIC to JPG on Mac for free?

JPEG uses lossy compression, so image quality can decrease depending on the export settings.
